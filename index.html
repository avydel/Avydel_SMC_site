Frequently asked questions

Do I need MetaTrader 5?
Yes. The assistant runs on Windows alongside MetaTrader 5 and reads live and historical data from your MT5 terminal. A demo or live MT5 account from any broker works.
Does this place trades for me automatically?
It highlights the setups and can auto-manage an open position (break-even, partials, trailing) on rules you set. You stay in control of whether and when to enter.
What happens after the 15-day free trial?
Nothing is charged automatically. When the trial ends you can switch to the Free 1-asset plan or pick a paid plan whenever you are ready.
Can I move my licence to a new PC?
Yes. Your licence is tied to one device at a time and can be transferred to a new PC. A small transfer fee may apply — just reach out to support.
Is this a signal service or financial advice?
No. It is an analysis and trade-management tool. It does not tell you what will happen in the market and it is not financial advice. You make every trading decision yourself.
How do I get updates?
Updates are delivered to your licensed app. Paid plans include ongoing updates; Premium VIP includes all future updates for life.
